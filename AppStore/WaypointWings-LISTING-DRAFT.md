Status: source-backed working draft for iOS 1.0 (`com.isaiahdupree.waypointwings`); not saved to App Store Connect. Reviewed 2026-10-22 against the current repository and public U.S. App Store listings.

## Proposed metadata

**Name (14/30):** Waypoint Wings  
**Subtitle (25/30):** Fly Twelve Gates Each Day  
**Candidate category:** Games / Action; validate the available ASC category taxonomy.  
**Promotional text (96/170):** Fly today's sunny island course. Clear twelve gates, use your boost, and chase your best flight.  
**Keywords (97/100 ASCII bytes):** airplane,flight,aviation,arcade,sky,archipelago,steering,boost,course,challenge,ghost,leaderboard

**Description:**

Fly the daily skyway through a sunny archipelago.

Each UTC day brings a generated course shared by every player. Guide your plane with the two-axis flight stick, clear twelve ordered gates, and use BOOST when you need extra speed. Release the stick to auto-level. A collision or missed course restarts the same attempt.

Keep your local flight history and try again to beat your best. When the leaderboard service is configured and reachable, compare daily flights and replay ghost paths. Offline flights remain playable; the game reports when online results cannot sync.

## Market evidence and positioning

Waypoint Wings should differentiate on features verified in its own source: a generated sunny archipelago, one shared UTC-day course, twelve ordered gates, two-axis flight control, boost, and local personal-best replays.
